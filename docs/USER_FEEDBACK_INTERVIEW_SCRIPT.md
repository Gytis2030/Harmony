# User Feedback Interview Script

Use this script for 1:1 sessions with producers, artists, and mix engineers during private beta. The goal is not to hear "it's cool" — it's to understand whether Harmony fits into a real workflow and whether someone would pay for it.

Keep the conversation flowing. You don't have to ask every question. If someone goes deep on one answer, follow it. Mark answers that are surprising or specific — those are the ones to act on.

---

## Before the Demo

Ask these before showing the product. You want uninfluenced context about their current workflow.

**Current tools and pain points**

1. What do you currently use to share music with collaborators or clients for feedback?
2. Walk me through how that works end to end — from when you have a draft mix to when you get feedback back.
3. Where does that workflow break down? What's the most frustrating part?
4. How do you manage different versions of a track? What happens when you need to go back?
5. Have you ever lost work or mixed up versions? What happened?

**Collaboration specifics**

6. How many people are usually involved in one project at the same time?
7. Do your collaborators use the same DAW as you, or is it mixed?
8. When you send a mix for feedback, what format do you use — stems, bounce, WeTransfer link?

---

## During / After the Demo

Show the product now. Run through the demo guide. Then ask:

**Reaction and fit**

9. When I played that back with the comments pinned to timestamps — does that map to how you think about feedback?
10. What confused you? Was anything unexpected?
11. What felt most valuable to you?
12. Is there anything you expected to see that wasn't there?

**Would you use it?**

13. If Harmony was available right now, would you use it on your next real collaboration — not a test project, a real one?
14. What would stop you from using it on that project?
15. Which feature is missing before this becomes genuinely useful to you?

---

## Pricing and Willingness to Pay

These are the most important questions. Ask them directly. Don't apologise for asking about money.

16. What do you currently spend per month on music production tools? (DAW, plugins, cloud storage, etc.)
17. Would you pay €10/month for this as a solo producer?
18. Would you pay €25/month for a team or studio workspace where you could have multiple projects and collaborators?
19. Is there a price where this is a no-brainer? What about a price where you'd walk away?

---

## Social Proof and Referral Potential

20. Who else would you invite into a project if you started using this today?
21. Would you recommend this to another producer you know? What would you tell them?
22. Are there any communities — Discord servers, forums, studios — where you think producers talk about this kind of problem?

---

## Closing

23. On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to use this in the next month — 10 being you're signing up today?
24. What would need to change to make that a 10?
25. Is there anything else you want to tell me that I didn't ask?
